# Clan Wars

## Overview
Asynchronous clan warfare with 24-48 hour duration.

## Requirements
- Min 5 members per clan
- Min clan level 3
- Cooldown between wars
- Power difference limit

## Scoring
- Win: 100 base points
- Loss: 20 base points
- Underdog bonus: +50
- Defensive win bonus: +30
- Diminishing returns on repeated matchups

## Attacks
- Max 5 per player
- 30-minute cooldown
- Cannot repeat defender

## Small Clan Protection
- Novice protection (7 days)
- Max wars per week: 5
- Decline cooldown: 60 minutes
- Balanced matchmaking

## Rewards
- Winner: 500 clan XP, 100 tokens, 1 territory point
- Loser: 100 clan XP, 25 tokens
- Individual rewards scaled by contribution
